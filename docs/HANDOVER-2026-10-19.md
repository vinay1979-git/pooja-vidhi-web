## 3. Decisions I need from you

### 3.1 Navaratri rebuild (0062) — the big one
Details and the full step table: `docs/plans/navaratri-rebuild-0062.md`.
1. **Length.** Following the book's rubric literally, every day repeats the full
   Purvanga, including the Vighneshwara pooja (27 screens). Day 1 = 109
   screens, days 2–9 = 99. Each screen is small and you only press Next, but it
   is long. A shorter daily form would need a source that allows it.
2. **Anga Vandanam** now uses the book's twelve bare names; other rites still
   say "…aaya namaha". Bring them in line?
3. **Prana Pratishtha** is the book's full devi-column nyasa (8 screens); the
   nine welcoming gestures are drawn but never described in the book.

### 3.2 Hand positions (0064) — what the book does not say
`docs/review/hand-positions.md` §(4) of each action. The main ones: which hand
rings the bell; the camphor waving pattern; whether the akshatai sits in the
left palm in the Sankalpam; touching the kalasham at Prana Pratishtha; joined
or cupped palms at pradakshina.

### 3.3 The three daily rites (0063)
`docs/plans/nitya-karmas-0063.md`. The book prints **no English gloss** for
these pages, so every meaning was written here and needs a reviewer, especially
the tarpanam lines. Also: Brahma Yajnam got a new deity row (`brahma`) only to
satisfy a check; *Urjam vahanteeh* is said only by those whose father has
passed (kept as one step with that instruction); evening Samidadhanam faces
west. Yajnopaveeta Dharana is transcribed but not published — recommended as a
rite of its own.

### 3.4 Guided mode (the Vidvaan Pandit voice)
The test harness is ready: `scripts/tts-bakeoff/README.md`. Put
`GEMINI_API_KEY` and `SARVAM_API_KEY` in `.env.local`, run
`node scripts/tts-bakeoff/run.mjs`, open `scripts/tts-bakeoff/out/ab.html`,
rate the clips blind (ideally someone trained in Vedic recitation), send back
the export. ~147 clips, estimated $0.09. No provider has been called yet.

### 3.5 Visuals
`docs/plans/step-visuals.md`. Approve the approach (one drawn figure + hand
poses, animated in the app; Nano Banana only to draft art from the book's own
drawings) and who reviews ritual accuracy.
