Sanskrit voice bake-off

Each mantra below was read by several synthetic voices. They are labelled A, B, C… in a random order, so you do not know which is which. Listen to each one, then rate it. Your ratings are kept in this browser; press Download when you are done and send the file back.

  • Pronunciation 1–5: 5 = every syllable correct (aspirates, long vowels, visarga, ṛ, conjuncts, no dropped "a").
  • Voice 1–5: 5 = sounds like a calm, reverent pandit you would follow in a pooja.
  • Mistakes: one per line, "word — what was wrong", e.g. धीमहि — said "dhimhi".
